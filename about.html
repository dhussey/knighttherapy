Meet Stephanie

Twenty-two years of sitting with clients through their hardest moments — and helping them find their footing again.

Stephanie Knight, MA

Counseling Psychology

  • MA, Counseling Psychology — Lewis & Clark College, 2005
  • Drug & Alcohol Certification, 2007
  • Private practice in Lake Oswego since 2008
  • 20+ years using DBT with clients

My name is Stephanie Knight, and I've had the utmost privilege of seeing hundreds of clients over the past 22 years. I'm truly passionate about the work I do, which I hope you'll discover when you meet me. You will never be a "number," just another client in my day, or a set of symptoms. I'll be "in it" with you for as long as you need my help, and I will meet you where you are.

I'm grateful for my years of experience in various settings. During college, I volunteered for four years at domestic violence shelters, where I supported women seeking safety and starting a new life, met women in hospitals to support them after a sexual assault, and worked the 24-hour women's crisis line — sometimes overnight, which likely wasn't my roommate's favorite part of living with me.

From there, I worked at an alternative high school and implemented behavioral modification for teens who had been expelled from Portland public schools. That work gave me the skills to reach clients who were resistant to help, along with a real understanding of the complicated reasons behind their struggles to trust themselves and others. I loved working in a school because I got to be part of the teens' day-to-day lives.

My next setting was a community mental health center, where I worked as a skills trainer for children and teens facing mental health struggles. I worked there throughout graduate school at Lewis & Clark College, and completed my practicum at another alternative high school, where I served as the school counselor.

Since earning my master's degree in counseling psychology, I've worked as an elementary school counselor, a drug and alcohol counselor — including with women in the Drug Court program — and at another community mental health center, where I saw clients from ages 5 to 75. I've been practicing privately in Lake Oswego since 2008.

When I'm not working, I'm spending time with friends and family, including my four daughters. I also enjoy running, cooking, climbing, traveling, or just about any big or little adventure.
