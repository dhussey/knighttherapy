FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)?

Practical life coping skills to help manage or prevent overwhelming emotions, with a focus on mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation, and interpersonal effectiveness.

What is Cognitive Behavior Therapy (CBT)?

The process of understanding how your emotions affect your thoughts, and how those thoughts affect your behaviors.

What is Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)?

The process of helping clients get unstuck from ruminating thoughts, define their values, and work toward intentionally living according to those values.

What is Emotion Focused Family Therapy (EFFT)?

EFFT gives caregivers practical tools for connecting with a loved one who's struggling with their mental health — including how to "emotion coach," as well as what issues you might bring to the table that affect your relationship with them.

What is Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR)?

EMDR helps clients get unstuck from their big or little traumas.

What is Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT)?

The process of getting unstuck from big or little traumas by challenging the stuck points that resulted from them.

What is Motivational Interviewing (MI)?

Asking questions in order to increase the likelihood of change.

What is a Strength-Based Approach?

Building on the strengths you already have in order to create change.
