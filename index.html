Feeling overwhelmed?

Stuck in problematic coping patterns? Do your emotions get the best of you?

Life, without any major disruptions, is stressful enough — but almost everyone goes through times that are especially difficult. People often find themselves getting lost, anxious, or frustrated during these times, and can develop problematic ways of coping simply because they don't know what else to do. That's understandable. Sometimes it means some internal dialogue and ruminating. Sometimes it means everything is falling apart, and things are bad — really bad.

This is where I can help. You can get unconditional support (no judgment here) and evidence-based solutions to your problems, so you can live more of the fulfilling, peaceful, and confident life you deserve.

How I work

Skills for today, without ignoring the past

I use only evidence-based practices to help you reach your goals — including my most recognized specialty, DBT, for emotion regulation. I also use EMDR for trauma recovery, along with ACT, CBT, and CPT for getting unstuck from difficult thoughts, and EFFT for reducing a loved one's mental health symptoms through practical communication strategies. I believe in looking back at the past when it's useful, while also equipping you to manage your emotions today. Over time, I'll give you many skills so you — or your loved one — can feel more confident managing emotions.
